Welcome to my blog. This is where I will share notes about development, creative projects, and things I learn along the way.

## How this blog works

Every article is a Markdown file stored with the website source code. When the site is published, the articles are bundled into the Blog automatically.

That means the posts are:

- available on every device;
- versioned with Git;
- hosted for free with the website; and
- easy to back up or move elsewhere.
